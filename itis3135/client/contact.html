Frequently Asked Questions

How can I report a bug in your games? ▼

Please report bugs through our Discord server in the #bug-reports channel, or email us at support@hivemind.com. Include as much detail as possible about the issue, including screenshots or videos if available.

Are you accepting new game ideas or collaboration proposals? ▼

Yes! We're always interested in hearing about collaboration opportunities and partnership ideas. Please reach out to partnerships@hivemind.com with a detailed proposal and we'll get back to you.

How often do you release game updates? ▼

Update frequency varies by game, but we generally release significant content updates quarterly, with smaller patches and bug fixes monthly. Major seasonal events are typically released 3-4 times per year.

Are you hiring developers or artists? ▼

We're frequently looking for talented individuals! Check our Discord or send your portfolio to careers@hivemind.com. We're always interested in hearing from passionate developers, artists, and designers.

How do I get help if I'm stuck in one of your games? ▼

Our Discord community is very helpful! Post your question in the game's support channel. You can also check our YouTube channel for tutorial videos, or email support@hivemind.com for technical issues.
