About Hivemind Entertainment

We are a passionate team of developers, artists, and designers committed to creating memorable gaming experiences. Founded in 2019, we've grown from a small indie studio to a dynamic team working on multiple Roblox projects simultaneously.

What We Do

  • Community Building: We foster vibrant communities around our games, ensuring player feedback shapes our development.
  • Innovation: We constantly explore new mechanics and technologies to deliver fresh experiences.
  • Collaboration: We work with industry partners and collaborative studios to bring ambitious projects to life.
