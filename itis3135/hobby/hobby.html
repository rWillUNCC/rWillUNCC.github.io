The Theaters of War

Maps range from the mountains of North Korea to the industrial zones of Scandinavia. The game is mostly set in real places on the Korean Peninsula. The most popular map, Wonsan Harbor, is based off the real harbor located at Wonsan. The variaty of maps is large offering different challenges.

Winning requires mastering different environments:

  • Urban: High infantry focus.
  • Open Fields: Tank dominance.
  • Forrest: Both tanks and infantry focus.
  • Coastal: Combined naval and air ops.

Logistics & Tactics

To succeed, you must manage your unit. Without supply trucks, your multi-million dollar tanks are just paperweights. Without infantry your lines are nothing. Artillery support adds dignity to where there would otherwise be an ugly brawl. Tanks can roll over opposing fronts. Vehicles can burn out the enemy. Helicopters provide crucial reconnaissance and support. Planes rule the skies and hunt the ground.

  1. Build a balanced deck with Recon, Infantry, and Support.
  2. Deploy your ground units.
  3. Protect your command units and base.
  4. Send your units to fight and win.

The Thrill of the Win

There is no other game that captures the tactical feel of modern warfare quite like this. Every loss is a lesson, and every win feels earned. When you play against others you learn how people behave. You can come to learn what causes them to tick. You can use that to your advantage to win. You can use this insight to improve your strategy in game and real life.

The Ultimate Cold War RTS

Wargame: Red Dragon is a real-time strategy game that simulates a massive conflict between NATO and Warsaw Pact forces. It features over 1,400 units across multiple nations. It takes place in a fictionalized version of what a second Korean War might have looked like. It offers an immersive experience with its real time tatical gameplay unquie to its genre.

  • Massive scale tactical combat.
  • In-depth deck building system.
  • Authentic 1975-1995 military hardware.
