Apex Predator Logic

Sharks have survived for over 400 million years using highly efficient biological algorithms.

To simulate shark movement, we can use the following logic:

if (preyDetected) { swimFast(); }

Their evolutionary success is a testament to their streamlined design.

Dermal Denticles

Unlike bony fish, shark skin is covered in tiny V-shaped scales called dermal denticles.

These scales reduce drag and turbulence, allowing the shark to swim more quietly and quickly.

float dragCoefficient = 0.02f;
  • Reduces fluid friction
  • Protects against parasites
  • Provides structural armor

Ampullae of Lorenzini

These are special sensing organs called electroreceptors.

They allow sharks to detect the electromagnetic fields emitted by all living creatures.

sensor.readMicrovolts(target_heartbeat);

This "sixth sense" is vital for hunting in dark or murky waters.

Buoyancy Control

Sharks do not have swim bladders like most fish. Instead, they rely on a massive, oil-filled liver.

liver.oil_density < water.density;

They also use their pectoral fins like airplane wings to create lift while swimming.

  • Constant movement required
  • Squalene-rich liver oil

Species Classification

There are over 500 species of sharks currently identified by marine biologists.

The classification can be viewed as an array of objects in our database:

const species = ["Great White", "Hammerhead", "Whale Shark", "Wobbegong"];

Each species fills a specific ecological niche in the ocean's hierarchy.
